Cookies Policy

Last updated: April 2025

Zaffre Soil Almanac uses a minimal set of cookies to keep the site running smoothly and to understand how readers use our content. We don't track you across the internet, and we never sell your data.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are tiny text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your actions and preferences (like language or font size) over time, so you don't have to re-enter them every time you browse. They also help us see which articles are most useful to our readers.

2. How we use cookies

We use a very small number of cookies, grouped into two simple categories:

  • Essential cookies: These are necessary for the site to function. They let you navigate between pages, use our contact form, and access secure areas. Without these, the site simply won't work properly.
  • Analytics cookies (privacy‑focused): We use a lightweight, anonymized analytics service to count visits and see which pages are popular. This data is aggregated and never linked to you personally. It helps us decide what to write about next.

We do not use advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or any third‑party cookies that follow you around the web.

3. Specific cookies we set

Here's a transparent list of the cookies you may encounter on our site:

  • zaffre_session — Essential. Keeps your session active while you browse. Expires when you close your browser.
  • zaffre_prefs — Essential. Stores small preferences like whether you've dismissed our cookie notice. Lasts 6 months.
  • zaffre_analytics — Analytics. A first‑party cookie that records a random ID to distinguish unique visits. No personal data. Lasts 12 months.

Our analytics provider may also set a cookie, but it is configured to anonymize IP addresses and does not share data with any other service.

4. Third‑party cookies

We do not embed third‑party services that set their own cookies (like YouTube, Facebook, or Google Maps). If we ever link to an external site, that site will have its own cookie policy — please check it separately.

5. Your control over cookies

You can control and delete cookies as you wish. Most browsers allow you to:

  • See what cookies you have and delete them individually.
  • Block third‑party cookies.
  • Block cookies from specific sites.
  • Block all cookies from being set.
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser.

Be aware that if you block or delete our essential cookies, some parts of the site (like the contact form) may not work correctly. You can find instructions for managing cookies in your browser's help section — here are links for Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.

🌿 A note on "Do Not Track"

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) setting. Because there is no common industry standard for how to respond, we treat all visitors the same: we only use the minimal, privacy‑respecting cookies described above. We do not track you across third‑party websites.

6. Changes to this policy

If we change our cookie usage in any meaningful way, we'll update this page and revise the "Last updated" date. We'll also notify newsletter subscribers if the change is significant.

7. More information

For more details about how we handle your personal information, please see our Privacy Policy. If you have any questions about cookies or this policy, reach out to us at privacy@zaffresoilalmanac.example.

We believe in keeping things simple — no confusing jargon, no hidden trackers. Just a clean, respectful website for gardeners.

Questions about cookies? We're happy to explain.
