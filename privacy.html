Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

Zaffre Soil Almanac is an independent gardening publication. We take your privacy seriously — this policy explains what we collect, why, and how we protect it. We keep things simple and transparent.

1. What information we collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide and basic technical data to keep the site running.

  • Contact form data: When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message content. This is used solely to reply to your inquiry.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: If you subscribe to The Dirt, we collect your email address. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in each email.
  • Server logs: Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically logs your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This data is anonymized and used for security and performance monitoring.
  • Cookies: We use minimal, functional cookies to remember your preferences and for basic analytics (see our Cookies Policy for details).

2. How we use your information

We use your data only for the purposes you'd expect:

  • To respond to your emails, pitches, or corrections.
  • To send you our newsletter if you've opted in.
  • To improve our content and site experience (anonymized analytics).
  • To protect against spam, fraud, and abuse.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Period.

3. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Consent: When you sign up for our newsletter or submit a contact form.
  • Legitimate interest: For basic site security, analytics, and responding to messages.
  • Legal obligation: Where we must retain records for legal reasons.

4. Data retention

We keep your information only as long as needed:

  • Contact form messages: retained for 12 months, then deleted.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: retained until you unsubscribe, plus a short period for suppression lists.
  • Server logs: anonymized and rotated every 30 days.

5. Your rights

Depending on your location, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent (e.g., unsubscribe from the newsletter).
  • Object to processing or request data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@zaffresoilalmanac.example. We'll respond within 30 days.

🌱 A note on third‑party services

We use a privacy‑focused analytics platform (no cross‑site tracking) and a reputable email service provider for our newsletter. These providers are contractually bound to protect your data and never use it for their own marketing.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any ad‑tech trackers.

6. Children's privacy

Our content is suitable for all ages, but we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. International transfers

Our servers are located in the United States. If you are accessing our site from outside the US, please be aware that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the US. We take steps to ensure your data receives adequate protection.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date at the top. If changes are significant, we'll notify newsletter subscribers directly.

This policy was written in plain language because we believe privacy shouldn't require a law degree. If you have questions, just contact us — a real person will reply.

Questions about your data? We're happy to help.
