    Zaffre Soil Almanac is an independent, reader‑supported journal. No ads, no sponsored fluff. Just practical, field‑tested knowledge for people who grow things.

    Our story

    Zaffre Soil Almanac started in 2021 as a shared notebook between four friends who kept answering the same gardening questions over and over. “How do I fix my clay soil?” “Why are my tomatoes splitting?” “When should I prune?” We realised that most gardening advice online was either too vague, too commercial, or just plain wrong.

    So we began writing the kind of articles we wished we could find: rooted in soil science, tested in real gardens, and written in plain language. We’re not a big media company. We’re a small team of market gardeners, horticulturists, and one stubborn soil nerd who reads academic papers for fun. Every article is written by someone who has actually done the work.

    “The best gardening advice is local. We try to give you principles, not just recipes.” — Marcus, Editor

    Today, over 8,000 gardeners read our weekly almanac. We remain fully independent, supported entirely by readers like you. That means we can write honestly about what works — and what doesn’t.

    How we work

    We don’t take advertising, sponsored posts, or free products in exchange for reviews. Our only allegiance is to our readers — and to the soil.

    Field‑tested

    Every technique we recommend has been used in our own gardens or on the farms we work with. No armchair gardening.

    Science‑informed

    We read the research, talk to extension agents, and update articles when new evidence emerges. We cite our sources where it matters.

    Reader‑supported

    No ads, no sponsored content. Our newsletter and optional donations keep the lights on. That’s it.

    Honest about failure

    We share what didn’t work, too. Gardening is full of variables, and pretending otherwise doesn’t help anyone.

    Transparency & contact

    We’re a small operation, and we like hearing from readers. If you spot an error, have a question, or want to suggest a topic, email us at hello@zaffresoilalmanac.example. We read everything, though we can’t always reply individually.

    Common questions

    Are you affiliated with any gardening brands?
    No. We don’t have affiliate links, sponsorships, or brand partnerships. If we mention a tool or product, it’s because we use it and find it useful — never because we’re paid to.
    How can I support the Almanac?
    The best way is to subscribe to our newsletter and share articles with fellow gardeners. We also accept optional donations via our support page. Every contribution goes directly to paying writers and keeping the site ad‑free.
    Can I republish your articles?
    We’re happy for you to quote short excerpts with a link back to the original. For full republishing, please email us first — we usually say yes for non‑profits and educational use.
    Do you accept guest posts?
    Occasionally, if the writer has hands‑on experience and a clear voice. Pitch us at hello@zaffresoilalmanac.example with a short outline and a link to your work.
