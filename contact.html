Before you write…

Do you accept guest posts?
We occasionally publish guest articles from experienced growers. Send a short pitch (2–3 paragraphs) to editors@zaffresoilalmanac.example. We do not accept sponsored posts.
Can I republish an article?
Please email us first. We sometimes allow republishing with credit and a link back — but we need to approve it in writing.
Where do you get your gardening advice?
Our writers are market gardeners, soil scientists, and longtime home growers. We blend field experience with peer-reviewed research whenever possible.
I’d like to send a physical letter or sample.
We love mail! Use the PO Box above. For seed or soil samples, please contact us first to arrange details.
