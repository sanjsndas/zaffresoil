    Privacy Policy

    We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

    1. Information we collect

    We keep data collection to a minimum. Here’s what we may collect:

    • Voluntary information: If you subscribe to our newsletter or contact us, we collect your email address and any information you choose to share.
    • Usage data: Like most websites, we use server logs and privacy‑respecting analytics to understand how visitors use the site (e.g., pages viewed, time on site). This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.
